Add :func:`~.utils.eds.get_k_factors` to compute Cliff-Lorimer k-factors (and
raw cross-section ratios) from X-ray emission cross-section tables at a given
beam energy (100, 200 or 300 keV), using the same X-ray line names as elsewhere
in eXSpy (``Ka``, ``Kb``, ``La``, ``Lb1``, ...). Lines of the same element can
be combined (e.g. ``Ka`` + ``Kb``) into a single whole-shell k-factor. See
:ref:`eds_quantification-label`.

``EDSTEMSpectrum.quantification`` with ``method="CL"`` now also accepts
``factors="standardless"``: the k-factors are then computed automatically from
the bundled tables for the beam energy stored in the microscope parameters,
matched to the X-ray lines of the given intensities, and the factors used are
recorded in the ``Sample.quantification_factors`` metadata of the returned
signals. The beam energy now also accepts float values, as stored in the
microscope metadata.
