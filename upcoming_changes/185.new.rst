Add :func:`~.utils.eds.get_k_factors` to compute Cliff-Lorimer k-factors (and
raw cross-section ratios) from X-ray emission cross-section tables at a given
beam energy (100, 200 or 300 keV), using the same X-ray line names as elsewhere
in eXSpy (``Ka``, ``Kb``, ``La``, ``Lb1``, ...). Lines of the same element can
be combined (e.g. ``Ka`` + ``Kb``) into a single whole-shell k-factor. See
:ref:`eds_quantification-label`.

``EDSTEMSpectrum.quantification`` with ``method="CL"`` now also accepts
``factors="standardless"``: the k-factors are then computed automatically from
the bundled tables for the beam energy stored in the microscope parameters,
matched to the X-ray lines of the given intensities, and the factors used are
recorded in the ``Sample.quantification_factors`` metadata of the returned
signals. The beam energy now also accepts float values, as stored in the
microscope metadata.

``get_k_factors`` and ``EDSTEMSpectrum.quantification`` (with
``factors="standardless"``) accept a ``detector_efficiency`` argument to
correct the standardless k-factors for the detection efficiency of the
detector, for quantification from raw intensities: each factor is multiplied
by the ratio of the (cross-section-weighted) efficiency of the reference line
to that of the corresponding line. The efficiency can be given as a callable
of the energy, a two-column (energy, efficiency) file or array (see the new
``load_detector_efficiency``), or calculated from the detector layers (see
the new ``detector_efficiency_from_layers``). The interpolated efficiency
curve of a SuperX windowless silicon drift detector is bundled with eXSpy
(``SUPERX_EFFICIENCY_FILE``), redistributed from the `ASTORUM
<https://github.com/sebastian-cozma/ASTORUM>`_ package.
